--- PAGE 20 ---
Cross-Modality Insights
j Think vs. No-Think
Small models (135M, 360M, 0.5B) never generate <think> to-
kens; only Gemma and TinyLlama can. 135M peaks 90% in
Think config but by exploiting other rewards, not reasoning.
Think doesn’t help for these tasks.
 Curriculum = Safety Net
Standard causes Gemma-2-2B to regress (−1.67pp). Curricu-
lum flips this to +0.67pp. Produces the absolute highest
scores (TinyLlama 99.33%, Qwen 98.00%).
  Inverted-U Gain Pattern
Smallest models gain most: 135M +58pp. Mid-range (360M,
1.1B): +13–26pp. Already-strong (0.5B, 2B): +0–8pp, approach-
ing ceiling.
. Hard Tier Drives Improvement
Baselines: 17–20% hard pass for 135M. Post-GRPO: 86–99% on
hard prompts. Simple saturates to 100% very early (Stage 1).

--- PAGE 21 ---
Lessons & Limitations
¥ Technical Lessons
• Additive rewards > gating
Zero-advantage collapse if all tie →vanishing gradient
• Graduated partial credit
Binary validity gives no signal for near-valid JSON
• 4-bit NF4 + LoRA sufficient
<2% params trained, single GPU, identical hyperparams
• Weight redistribution
Auto-preserves component ratios when Think off
- Limitations
• JSON only — not directly
generalizable to other formats
• Fully synthetic dataset;
potential real-world gap
• No comparison with DPO/PPO
(only GRPO tested)
• Fixed hyperparameters —
per-model tuning could help
21/22
