[TEXT_BOX (17)] UCF-101 Dataset
[TEXT_BOX (17)] • 101 action classes (sports, daily activities)
• 13,320 video clips from YouTube
• Sampled at 24 frames/clip (112×112 crop)
• Official test split: 3,783 clips
[TEXT_BOX (17)] Group-Aware Eval Split
[TEXT_BOX (17)] UCF-101 clips are grouped by source video.
A naïve random split leaks correlated frames
across train/eval → inflated val metrics.

• Split at group level (regex on video ID)
• 80% train / 20% internal eval — no leakage
• Official test set reserved for final evaluation
• Only group-aware results used in this work
[TEXT_BOX (17)] Training Configuration
[TEXT_BOX (17)] Student (MobileNet3D)
  Optimizer: AdamW   WD: 0.01
  LR: 5e-4   Scheduler: cosine annealing
  Epochs: 60   Batch: 16   AMP ✓
  KD warmup: 10 epochs (CE-only)
  Label smoothing: 0.1

Teacher (ResNet-50)
  Pretrained: Kinetics-400
  Optimizer: SGD   LR: 7e-4
  Epochs: 50   Batch: 12
  Fine-tuned on UCF-101

[TEXT_BOX (17)] Shared Challenges (Persistent)
[TEXT_BOX (17)] • HandstandWalking and Nunchucks remain in the top 5 errors for both models:
  - HandstandWalking: 2.94% → 5.88% (+2.94 pp)
  - Nunchucks: 11.43% → 20.00% (+8.57 pp)
  - MoppingFloor: 11.76% → 20.59% (+8.82 pp)
• Complex dynamics: These classes feature rapid, complex body motion sequences that remain hard to capture.
• Persistent confusions: MoppingFloor is heavily confused with HandstandWalking in both (26.5% vs 29.4%).
[TEXT_BOX (17)] Greatest Successes (KD Resolution)
[TEXT_BOX (17)] • KD regularizes baseline's worst classes by providing soft inter-class targets:
  - WallPushups: 0.00% → 65.71% (+65.71 pp)
  - HeadMassage: 12.20% → 41.46% (+29.26 pp)
  - JumpRope: 10.53% → 23.68% (+13.16 pp)
• Teacher soft logits guide the student through subtle motion boundaries.
• Clear confusions: JumpRope confusion with JumpingJack falls from 18.4% to 0%.
[TEXT_BOX (17)] Unexpected Regressions (Trade-offs)
[TEXT_BOX (17)] • Distillation degrades classes requiring fine-grained or fast periodic cues:
  - Hammering: 12.12% → 3.03% (−9.09 pp)
  - PizzaTossing: 27.27% → 15.15% (−12.12 pp)
  - Archery: 29.27% → 19.51% (−9.76 pp)
• Over-smoothing: At T=20, soft targets smooth out fine visual details (e.g. hammer/pizza shape).
• High confusion: Hammering gets confused with PlayingSitar (12.1%); Archery with WritingOnBoard (22.0%).

[TEXT_BOX (17)] ADVANCED METHODS — ATTENTION TRANSFER
[TEXT_BOX (17)] Attention Transfer (AT)
[TEXT_BOX (17)] Aligning spatial/temporal activation maps across network stages
[AUTO_SHAPE (1)] 
[TEXT_BOX (17)] Approach
[TEXT_BOX (17)] L2-normalized squared activation maps from matched
stages aligned between teacher (ResNet-50) and student.

L_total = L_KD + β_s·L_AT_spatial + β_t·L_AT_temporal
[TEXT_BOX (17)] Bug Fixed: Key-5 Error
[TEXT_BOX (17)] Initial config: block [3,4,5] of teacher.
Block 5 = classification head (2D) → constant
attention map at 1.0 → AT loss silently vanished.

Fix: mapping [2,3,4]→[2,4,6] (ResNet→MobileNet).
Stages share spatial resolutions: 28×28, 14×14, 7×7.

[TEXT_BOX (17)] CONCLUSIONS
[TEXT_BOX (17)] Summary & Key Findings
[AUTO_SHAPE (1)] 
[AUTO_SHAPE (1)] 
[TEXT_BOX (17)] Logit-Based KD
[TEXT_BOX (17)] Effective and zero-overhead. Best config: T=20, α=0.7 → 65.85% Top-1 (+6.37 pp vs. scratch baseline 59.48%). Higher T smooths logits and exposes inter-class structure.
[AUTO_SHAPE (1)] 
[TEXT_BOX (17)] Attention Transfer
[TEXT_BOX (17)] Limited by depthwise ↔ standard conv mismatch. Spatial AT over-constrains student; temporal-only AT is the best variant (62.07%). All AT variants underperform pure KD T=20.
[AUTO_SHAPE (1)] 
[TEXT_BOX (17)] Born-Again Self-Distillation
[TEXT_BOX (17)] Sensitive to teacher quality and temperature calibration. Noisy soft-targets from a ~65% teacher cause progressive generational collapse (64% → 59.21%).
[AUTO_SHAPE (1)] 
[TEXT_BOX (17)] Cross-Frame Distillation
[TEXT_BOX (17)] 16-frame Model B achieves 1.6× GPU speedup and crosses 100 ms CPU threshold (88.5 ms/clip). −3.17 pp accuracy vs. Model A — an excellent operating point for edge deployment.
[AUTO_SHAPE (1)] 
[TEXT_BOX (17)] Take-Away
[TEXT_BOX (17)] KD compresses a 3D ResNet-50 (~128 MB) into a MobileNet3D (~9.5 MB) achieving 65.85% Top-1 on UCF-101 (+6.37 pp over scratch). The 16-frame variant reduces inference by 37–40% on GPU with only −3.17 pp accuracy cost — an excellent operating point for real-world edge deployments.
