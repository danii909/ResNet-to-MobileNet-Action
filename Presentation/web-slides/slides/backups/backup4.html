Lessons Learned

The spatial capacity gap limits intermediate feature transfer. Logit distillation at high temperature ($T=20$) is the most stable guide.

Key Limitations

• Spatial capacity bottleneck of the Student.
• Over-smoothing on fast, periodic actions.
• Recursive collapse in self-distillation without oracle.

Future Work

Dynamic temperature calibration to prevent self-distillation collapse, and training on flexible temporal boundaries (variable stride).

Detailed Per-Class Analysis

Why some classes improve

Fine spatial details: WallPushups (0% ? 65.7%), HeadMassage benefit from Teacher's complex filters.

Background clutter removal: Distillation ignores irrelevant contexts (PlayingGuitar +27.9%).

Why some classes regress

Temporal smoothing: JumpingJack -16.2%, TennisSwing -14.3% — high T over-smooths transitions.

Capacity trade-off: Student sacrifices motion generalization when forced to imitate spatial features.
